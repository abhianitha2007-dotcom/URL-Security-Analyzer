Safe Browsing Tips

  • Check the registered domain, not only the page title.
  • Watch for spelling changes and number substitutions.
  • Avoid shortened links from unknown senders.
  • Never share passwords, OTPs or card PINs.
  • Use multi-factor authentication where available.
  • Open banking and government portals through official sources.
